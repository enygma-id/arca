# A-04 — Community/Enterprise rights matrix (internal administrative memo)

| Material | Community distribution | Potential Enterprise rights | Evidence/limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARCA-owned core | `AGPL-3.0-only` | Enygma may grant alternative terms to the extent rights are controlled by Enygma | User/company confirms human-led AI-assisted code; preserve assignment/engagement records |
| External contribution | AGPL under applicable merge/contribution terms | Alternative terms ONLY if enforceable CLA/assignment or independent permission applies | `CLA.md` is draft; no acceptance records supplied |
| MIT/BSD/Apache third-party components | Original terms apply alongside ARCA | Original terms still apply, attribution/notice/patents follow upstream | Enygma cannot relicense upstream copyright outright |
| GEOS LGPL/native components | Subject to verified LGPL and binary distribution facts | Same upstream LGPL obligations, regardless of Enterprise | Check physical wheel and static/dynamic/relink conditions |
| Proprietary Enterprise modules separately authored | Not automatically included in Community | Governed by separately signed enterprise contract | Do not invent downstream terms |
| Third-party APIs/CDN/maps | External provider rights/conditions | Separate provider agreements if different use required | BIG rights incomplete; OSM tile policy distinct from database license |
| User input BIM and generated artifacts | Under respective content/rightsholder terms, not automatically ARCA AGPL | Respect same user/data rights | Do not impose AGPL on GeoJSON/GLB solely due to processing |

This is not legal advice and not a substitute for Enygma/counsel execution of commercial licensing.
