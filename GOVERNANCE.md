# Governance

## Project

**ARCA: Asset Representation Conversion Architecture**

ARCA is founded and stewarded by **Enygma**.

Legal Project Steward:

**PT Enygma Solusi Negeri**

## Governance Principles

ARCA governance is intended to preserve:

- technical quality;
- scientific integrity;
- historical attribution;
- open-source continuity;
- dependency provenance;
- licensing integrity;
- security;
- reproducibility;
- official project identity.

## Project Steward

The Project Steward is responsible for:

- canonical repository designation;
- governance policy;
- release policy;
- licensing policy where legally authorized;
- Contributor License Agreement policy;
- official project identity and brand stewardship;
- maintainer appointment and removal;
- security escalation;
- institutional representation;
- major strategic direction.

Project stewardship does not reduce rights already granted under an applicable
open-source license.

## Roles

### Core Maintainers

Core Maintainers have review and merge authority over core ARCA areas.

### Area Maintainers

Area Maintainers may be appointed for domains such as:

- SKP/source adapters;
- IFC parsing;
- spatial reference;
- geometry normalization;
- GIS-native generalization;
- representation profiles;
- benchmark methodology;
- packaging and CLI;
- tests and validation;
- documentation;
- release engineering.

### Contributors

Contributors are people or legal entities whose work is accepted into the
official repository.

Contributor status does not automatically grant:

- merge authority;
- project stewardship;
- trademark rights;
- Enterprise licensing authority;
- authority to represent Enygma or ARCA institutionally.

## Decision-Making

ARCA seeks technical consensus where practical.

Escalation order:

1. Area Maintainers resolve ordinary implementation issues.
2. Core Maintainers resolve cross-area technical issues.
3. Designated technical/research leadership resolves major representation,
   geometry, or methodology disputes.
4. The Project Steward resolves governance, licensing, brand, canonical
   repository, institutional, and major strategic disputes.

## Scientific Integrity

Material algorithm or representation changes should document:

- motivation;
- assumptions;
- source-model expectations;
- parameter changes;
- expected behavior;
- validation method;
- known limitations;
- failure cases;
- reproducibility instructions.

Claims about LoD, geometry fidelity, semantic retention, georeferencing
accuracy, performance, or standards conformance should not be introduced
without sufficient evidence.

## Community and Enterprise Licensing

Covered Community Edition source is released under `AGPL-3.0-only`.

Alternative licensing may be offered only for material where
PT Enygma Solusi Negeri has sufficient legal rights.

External contributions may require an auditable CLA acceptance.
