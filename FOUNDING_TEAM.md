## Founding Sequence

ARCA began as an engineering question inside Enygma rather than as a branding
exercise.

The first working code was authored by Erick Karya to test whether complex BIM
and SketchUp-derived geometry could be transformed into GIS-native building
representations without forcing the downstream system to remain BIM-centric.

Hanafi then continued implementation and visualization work so the concept
could be exercised as a usable workflow rather than a one-off experiment.

LLM systems assisted parts of coding, debugging, comparison, and documentation,
but did not originate the project problem or replace human technical judgment.

See [`AI_USAGE.md`](./AI_USAGE.md) and
[`docs/project-history.md`](./docs/project-history.md).
