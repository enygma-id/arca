## Dataset creation statement

Enygma declares that the sample dataset was created and prepared by Enygma:

- `data/arca_synthetic_mall_10f.skp`: a synthetic SketchUp (.skp) commercial building model created by Enygma for ARCA research, format conversion testing, and reproducible BIM-to-GIS transformation experiments. No client BIM or proprietary building geometry was used.
- `data/arca_synthetic_mall_10f.json`: companion georeference metadata defining WGS 84 placement and projected CRS.
